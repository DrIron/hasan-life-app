Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Hasan Life is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Tasks and Google Calendar data solely to display and update that data for the owner.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
